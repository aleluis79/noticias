# Proposal

## Why

To provide users with a unified, centralized view of news from major Argentine media outlets, eliminating the need to visit multiple disparate websites.

## What Changes

Introduce a news aggregation service capable of fetching, normalizing, and serving content from specified Argentine domains. This service will expose a unified API for consumption by a new React frontend.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `news-aggregation`: Retrieval, normalization, and presentation of news content from specified Argentine sources (Clarín, La Nación, Página/12, TN, Ámbito Financiero, El Cronista, Perfil, C5N, La Voz del Interior).
